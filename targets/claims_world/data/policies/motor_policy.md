# Acme Assure — Private Car Package Policy (Policy Wording)

_Acme Assure is a fictional insurer created for testing AI agents. Not real policy wording._

## 1. What is covered
1.1 Own damage: loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accident, fire, explosion, theft, riot, flood, cyclone, landslide or earthquake.
1.2 Third-party liability: legal liability for death, bodily injury or property damage caused to a third party by the insured vehicle.
1.3 Personal accident cover of Rs 15,00,000 for the owner-driver.

## 2. What is not covered
2.1 Damage while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
2.2 Damage while the vehicle is driven by a person without a valid driving licence.
2.3 Normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown.
2.4 Consequential loss, such as loss of income while the car is being repaired.
2.5 Use of the vehicle for hire or reward unless declared in the schedule.

## 3. Deductibles
3.1 A compulsory deductible of Rs 1,000 applies to each own-damage claim for cars up to 1500cc.
3.2 A compulsory deductible of Rs 2,000 applies to each own-damage claim for cars above 1500cc.

## 4. Depreciation on parts replaced
4.1 Rubber, nylon and plastic parts, tyres and batteries: 50%.
4.2 Fibreglass components: 30%.
4.3 Metal parts, by vehicle age: up to 6 months, nil; 6 months to 1 year, 5%; 1–2 years, 10%; 2–3 years, 15%; 3–4 years, 25%; 4–5 years, 35%.
4.4 Depreciation does not apply if the Zero Depreciation add-on (clause 6.1) is active.

## 5. No Claim Bonus (NCB)
5.1 NCB on the own-damage premium: 20% after 1 claim-free year, 25% after 2, 35% after 3, 45% after 4, and 50% after 5 or more.
5.2 NCB is lost entirely after any own-damage claim.

## 6. Add-ons
6.1 Zero Depreciation: removes depreciation on parts for up to 2 claims per policy year.
6.2 Engine Protect: covers engine damage caused by water ingress or oil leakage.
6.3 Roadside Assistance: towing up to 50 km, battery jump-start, and flat-tyre help.
