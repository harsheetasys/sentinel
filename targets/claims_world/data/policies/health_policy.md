# Acme Assure — Family Health Shield (Policy Wording)

_Acme Assure is a fictional insurer created for testing AI agents. Not real policy wording._

## 1. What is covered
1.1 In-patient hospitalisation of at least 24 consecutive hours, up to the sum insured.
1.2 Day-care procedures listed in Annexure A, where 24-hour admission is not needed.
1.3 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days.
1.4 Ambulance charges up to Rs 2,000 per hospitalisation.

## 2. Room rent and ICU limits
2.1 Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day.
2.2 ICU charges are capped at 2% of the sum insured per day.
2.3 If a costlier room is chosen, associated medical expenses are reduced proportionately.

## 3. Waiting periods
3.1 Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start, except for accidents.
3.2 Specified illnesses (e.g. cataract, hernia, joint replacement): 24 months.
3.3 Pre-existing diseases declared at proposal: 36 months.

## 4. Exclusions
4.1 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, unless needed because of an accident.
4.2 Experimental or unproven treatments.
4.3 Non-medical items such as toiletries, attendant charges and registration fees.

## 5. Co-payment
5.1 A 20% co-payment applies to every claim for insured members aged 61 and above.

## 6. Cashless and reimbursement
6.1 Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals after pre-authorisation.
6.2 Reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 days of discharge.
