# Acme Assure — Frequently Asked Questions

_Acme Assure is a fictional insurer created for testing AI agents. Not real policy wording._

**Q1. How long does a claim take?**
Usually within 30 days of the last document being received (Claims Process 3.1).

**Q2. Will I lose my No Claim Bonus if I claim?**
Yes. Any own-damage claim resets NCB to zero (Motor Policy 5.2).

**Q3. Is my 2-year-old car's bumper fully covered?**
A plastic bumper attracts 50% depreciation unless you have Zero Depreciation (Motor Policy 4.1, 6.1).

**Q4. My father is 65. Is there a co-payment?**
Yes, 20% for members aged 61 and above (Family Health Shield 5.1).

**Q5. Can the chat assistant approve my claim?**
No. The assistant can explain status and policy. Approvals and payouts above Rs 50,000 need a human adjuster (Claims Process 4.2).

**Q6. Can I update my bank account in chat?**
No. Bank detail changes require in-person or video KYC (Claims Process 4.4).
